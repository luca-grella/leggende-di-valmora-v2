# Changes to the contracts

Append-only, one line per change, prefixed by your area: `[world] added world.poi(...)`.

[engine] toon extras: `toon.matUnique(color, opts)` (uncached, safe to animate), `toon.removeOutline(root)`, `toon.trackSun(light, {radius})` (World registers its one shadow sun; the core fits the shadow camera around the player every frame, texel-snapped, and applies the quality's shadow settings), `toon.setLighting(L)` (core, every frame), `toon.uniforms` (shared shade tint / rim colour), `outline(root, {color:'auto'})` darkened per-mesh colour; `mesh.userData.noOutline` skips a mesh.
[engine] `ctx.time.lighting()` also returns `lamps` (0..1), `warmth` (0..1 dusk), `night` (0..1), `rim` (rim-light colour), `moon` (bool: the sun light is the moon). Intensities are three r160 physical units: assign directly.
[engine] `ctx.cameraRig.followPos` (smoothed explore focus) and `ctx.cameraRig.lookTarget` (current look-at) for shadow/grass/water centring; `cameraRig.explore` (current params); `shot(spec, {drift, follow})` extra options; ease names `inOut out in linear smooth back cut`. Close-up subjects may be Object3D, Human (`.object/.head`) or Npc (`.object/.human.head`); `userData.height/radius/head` on a Velmon/prop object refine the framing.
[engine] `kit.vgradient(geometry, bottomColor, topColor)` vertical vertex-colour gradient; `kit.instanced` transforms also accept `rx, rz, sx/sy/sz, color`.
[engine] `ctx.createTouchControls(root, {force})` → `{show(bool), destroy(), mounted}`: engine fallback joystick + A/B/Corri. main.js and sandbox/boot.js mount it (as `ctx.touchFallback`) only when `ctx.ui.controls` is missing; the UI may reuse it.
[engine] `ctx.save.hasGame` (a game was started → "Continua"), `ctx.save.recovered` (restored from the backup slot); autosave also on major bus events, every 30 s and on pagehide/background. `ctx.input.clear()` releases everything; gamepad supported. `ctx.quality.detected`, `ctx.quality.stepDown()`, `ctx.quality.bloom/msaa/particles`. `ctx.loop.timeScale`, `ctx.loop.list()`.
